01 — the mark

The same geometry at every size. Nothing is a bitmap here: the brackets are computed from one set of constants, so what survives at 38 px is the structure, not a detail applied on top of it.

Why brackets

Machina's differentiator isn't answers, it's cited answers, and brackets are the universal mark of a reference.

The name comes from the Machine in Person of Interest — a system of total capture that surfaces exactly one relevant thing. The show renders the Machine's attention as bracket frames snapping onto a subject, so the mark is also the namesake's own visual signature.

It is the only mark in the exploration that says something true about the product at notification size.

02 — the indicator

The mark's job in the app is to replace the Ask thinking orb. The motion has to depict the operation rather than signal "busy" — so pick a motion and watch what each one asserts.

Clamp

Narrowing in. The calm default — the brackets never stop being brackets.

Step

Ratchets one tick per candidate. The only motion that can be made honest: bind the ticks to sources actually retrieved and it reports instead of mimes.

Trace

The reticle assembles. Its first frames are four loose corner ticks — the namesake's targeting frame, arrived at by accident. Best as the entry.

Rotation was tested and cut: at 90° the brackets become a top-and-bottom frame and mid-sweep they read as loose diagonals, so the mark stops being a citation for most of its loop.
